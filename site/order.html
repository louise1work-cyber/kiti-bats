How it works

Four steps, usually same day.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Product, quantity and the town you're in. If it's for a club or squad, say roughly how many players.

  2. We confirm the total

    Price, courier cost and lead time, all in one message. No surprises later.

  3. Payment

    We'll send you our banking details by EFT. Payment confirms the order.

  4. Delivered or collected

    Couriered to your door anywhere in South Africa, or collect in Johannesburg or Hartenbos.

Delivery

Anywhere in South Africa.

We courier countrywide, and courier is not included in the prices above. For bats it is roughly R50. For a Catch-It Trainer it runs R100 to R300, depending on your area. We confirm the exact figure before you pay.

Prefer to collect? You're welcome to fetch in Johannesburg from Nico, or in Hartenbos from Willie.

A note on lead times. Bats and Catch-It Trainers are usually in stock and go out quickly. If we are making a batch for a club we'll give you an honest date when you enquire, not an optimistic one.
